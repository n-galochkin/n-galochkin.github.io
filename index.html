Who I Am

10+ years · 6 titles shipped · AAA experience · Unreal & Unity

I have loved games for as long as I can remember. I grew up playing them — and dreaming of building my own. From my first clumsy attempts in school, through diplomas and a dissertation in game development, every step pointed at a single dream: to create games.

That dream has carried me down the path of game-making for more than ten years. I've fought alongside brilliant teams on projects I'm proud of, shaping gameplay, netcode and AI in Unreal Engine across PC, mobile and VR — and I still chase the very thing I did as a kid: to ship a Game of the Year.

So if you carry that same dream — I'm in.
